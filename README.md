# Franklean Cleaning Quote Calculator

A simple web-based quote calculator designed for **Franklean Cleaning** to provide estimated cleaning prices for bond cleaning and related services.

This project helps users quickly calculate an estimated starting price based on property details such as the number of bedrooms, bathrooms, carpet areas, pest control requirements, and additional cleaning conditions.

> **Note:** The final cleaning price may vary depending on the actual property condition. The final price should be confirmed after inspection or discussion with the customer.

---

## Features

* Calculate estimated bond cleaning prices
* Add property details such as:

  * Number of bedrooms
  * Number of bathrooms
  * Carpet cleaning option
  * Pest control option
  * Wall condition
  * Oven and rangehood condition
  * Extra rooms or special cleaning areas
* Display estimated starting price
* Show possible extra charge notices
* Simple and user-friendly interface
* Suitable for cleaning business quotation workflow

---

## Purpose of This Project

This project was created to support the daily quotation process for a cleaning business.

Instead of manually calculating every quote, the calculator provides a structured estimate based on predefined pricing logic. It can help reduce communication time, improve consistency, and provide customers with a clearer understanding of potential costs.

---

## Business Use Case

This quote calculator is designed for cleaning services such as:

* Bond cleaning
* End-of-lease cleaning
* Carpet steam cleaning
* Pest control
* Wall cleaning
* Oven cleaning
* Rangehood cleaning
* Window and track cleaning
* Extra condition-based cleaning charges

The calculator is especially useful when responding to customer enquiries through email, SMS, website forms, or social media.

---

## Important Pricing Disclaimer

All prices shown by this calculator are **estimated starting prices only**.

The final price may change depending on:

* Property size
* Actual condition of the property
* Level of dirt, dust, grease, mould, or pet hair
* Wall marks and scuff marks
* Oven and rangehood condition
* Window and track condition
* Carpet condition and carpet area
* Additional requests from the customer or property manager

Final price should be confirmed before starting the job.
